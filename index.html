Personal gog CLI

A private command-line tool that lets its owner read their own Gmail and prepare drafts from their own computer. It is built on the open-source gog Google Workspace CLI.

Who can use it

Only the account owner. It is not offered to the public and does not accept sign-ups.

What it does

  • Reads and searches the owner's Gmail (read-only access).
  • Creates and deletes drafts. It is configured never to send mail.
